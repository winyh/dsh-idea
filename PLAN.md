# dsh-idea plan

## Current scope

- Discover external market opportunities, unmet needs and “why now” signals from user-selected public sources.
- Scan bounded public HTTP(S) pages without cookies, credentials or unrestricted crawling.
- Normalize optional Markdown, CSV, TSV, JSON and JSONL pain-point signals as local cache/export inputs.
- Filter signals by keyword, scenario, target user, source, time and evidence strength.
- Rank signals by recency, repetition, pain and verifiability.
- Cluster signals into opportunity themes instead of treating each complaint as a feature.
- Draft candidate directions and the riskiest validation experiment.
- Recommend external source routes and search recipes for Product Hunt, TrustMRR, Reddit, G2, GitHub and trend sources.
- Score evidence by concrete behavior, workaround, cost, repetition, cross-source support, commercial signals and “why now”.
- Generate JTBD, Mom Test, switching and critical-event interview guides.
- Represent review results as an Opportunity Solution Tree with multiple solutions and experiments.
- Provide an onboarding/readiness check and a structured review result.
- Emit a versioned opportunity handoff that preserves evidence boundaries and the smallest validation experiment for dsh-product.

## Deliberate boundaries

- No unrestricted crawling of Reddit, Hacker News, GitHub or other communities; only user-selected public URLs are fetched.
- No claim that a pain signal proves demand, market size or willingness to pay.
- No live posting, outreach or collection of private credentials.
- Public source snapshots are bounded, dated and labeled with truncation/HTTP limitations.
- Local files are supplementary evidence, not the primary product boundary.

## Next iterations

1. Add optional adapters for user-approved public source exports and source-specific parsers.
2. Add watchlists and bounded snapshot diffs for user-selected public pages.
3. Add guarded Markdown preview/apply for opportunity briefs.
4. Add richer multilingual clustering while keeping source evidence visible.
